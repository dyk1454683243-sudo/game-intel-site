技能

  • Navigation Support basic — Summon Squeakie to shoot three shots in a row, dealing Electro DMG.
  • Searchlight Service skill — When in Yellow Light Mode, use Resonance Skill to perform Pounce, which consumes STA to pounce on the target before switching to Red Light Mode.
  • Squeakie Express liberation — Throw the giant Squeakie at the target, dealing Electro DMG.
  • Signal Light forte — When Yellow Light Spark is fully recovered, replace Resonance Skill with Resonance Skill Energized Pounce that deals Electro DMG and enter Red Spotlight Mode.
  • Pathfinding inherent — Gain 10% Electro DMG Bonus when in Red Light Mode.
  • Expediting inherent — Casting Energized Pounce or Energized Rebound increases ATK by 10% for 5s.
  • Special Delivery intro — Enter Yellow Light Mode and attack the target, dealing Electro DMG.
  • Escorting outro — The incoming Resonator has their Resonance Skill DMG Amplified by 38% for 10s or until they are switched out.
