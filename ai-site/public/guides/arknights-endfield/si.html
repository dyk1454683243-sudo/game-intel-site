祀 si

verified card

定位：辅助

技能

  • Artistic Urge basic — BASIC ATTACK: An attack with up to 5 sequences that deals Cryo DMG. As the controlled operator, Final Strike also deals 18 Stagger. DIVE ATTACK: Basic attack performed in mid-air becomes a dive attack that deals Cryo DMG to nearby enemies. FINISHER: Basic attack performed near a Stagger ed enemy becomes a finisher that deals massive Cryo DMG and recovers some SP.
  • Brushwork Realm battle — SP Cost: 100 Si deals Cryo DMG and applies Artful Intent, Immobilization, and Cryo Infliction (or Heat Infliction if a Heat element operator is in Team Position 01) on the target. After a target with Artful Intent takes DMG, part of the DMG taken is recorded. When the duration of Artful Intent ends, it bursts and deals Fixed DMG equal to the DMG recorded. After casting the skill, Si's battle skill is temporarily changed to Initial Stroke. Initial Stroke: Costs 15 SP. Si pulls an Inksoul from a target with Artful Intent. All DMG dealt and effects applied to the Inksoul will also be dealt to its host (the said target). The Inksoul lasts as long as the remaining duration of Artful Intent. After casting the skill, Si's battle skill is temporarily changed to Final Stroke. Final Stroke: Costs no SP. Makes the Inksoul disappear early. When disappearing, the Inksoul will move to its host and trigger a burst like the ending burst of Artful Intent. When dealing DMG or applying effects to both the Inksoul and the host, only 1 instance of the DMG or effect is dealt/applied. If the host is defeated during the Inksoul's duration, it will link to another enemy in combat and apply Artful Intent to it. This does not reset the duration of Artful Intent. If a target with Artful Intent is defeated without having its Inksoul drawn out of it, the defeated target will drop an Inksoul at its location.
  • Fully Envisioned combo — Cooldown: 15s Si summons an Ink Lung that deals Cryo DMG. This DMG is based on the highest stacks of Arts Infliction consumed or absorbed from a single target. Trigger: When an enemy's Cryo Infliction or Heat Infliction is consumed or absorbed.
  • No One Spoils the Fun ultimate — Energy cost: 80 Si summons a Giant Ink Lung from her painting that deals Cryo DMG and grants temporary Cryo Amp and Heat Amp to the entire team.
  • Ware is the Geist base — Assign to Reception Room to grant operator clue collecting efficiency +20/30%.
  • Ink Spirit Aide base — Assign to Reception Room to slow Mood Drop of all operators in it by 14/18%.
  • Proxy's Patronage talent — Ultimate skill No One Spoils the Fun improved: After casting, Team Position 01 operator's combo skill cooldown length -10/20s.
  • Unsoiled Brush talent — When the Inksoul disappears, allied operators gain 15/30% Haste for 5s.
