提弗洛斯 typhoeus

verified card

定位：突击

技能

  • Wildlands Huntress basic — BASIC ATTACK: An attack with up to 5 sequences that deals Nature DMG. As the controlled operator, Final Strike also deals 17 Stagger. DIVE ATTACK: Basic attack performed in mid-air becomes a dive attack that deals Nature DMG to nearby enemies. FINISHER: Basic attack performed near a Stagger ed enemy becomes a finisher that deals massive Nature DMG and recovers some SP.
  • Piercing Arrows of the Woods battle — SP Cost: 100 Typhoeus leaps into the air and enters a short Hovering state, during which she can unleash 5 Aerial Basic Attacks (ABATK). Each ABATK can shoot arrows at up to 2 targets and deal Nature DMG. Whenever an ABATK hits an enemy with Nature Infliction, it consumes 1 stack of Nature Infliction, grants Typhoeus 1 Sign, and gives additional Ultimate Energy. If Typhoeus has Hunting Arrows, she consumes 1 Hunting Arrow and converts the ABATK into a Power Shot. Power Shot: After hitting an enemy, Power Shot forcibly applies 1 instance of Nature Burst; if the target's Nature Infliction is consumed, then increase the DMG dealt by this Nature Burst to 1.3 times the original. If Typhoeus is the controlled operator, her 5th ABATK is regarded as a Final Strike and a Power Shot, and does not consume Hunting Arrows.
  • Rampant Thorns combo — Cooldown: 19s Transmutes all accumulated Signs to Hunting Arrows and resets her Aerial Basic Attack (ABATK) attempts. Typhoeus focuses her "Huntress's Gaze" and launches a barrage at the target that creates a Barrage Array at the target area. Enemies in the Barrage Array take Nature DMG over time, are slowed, and suffer increased Nature Burst DMG Taken. Every 2 Signs convert to 1 Hunting Arrow. Trigger: When Typhoeus reaches max Signs.
  • Call of Ice Mountain ultimate — Energy cost: 200 Typhoeus fires an Imbued Arrow that explodes after hitting the enemy and deals massive Nature DMG to enemies within the area of effect (AOE). She then enters a Hovering state and her Aerial Basic Attack (ABATK) attempts are reset. For her next 5 ABATKs, hitting an enemy triggers a Hail of Arrows that deals Nature DMG at the target's location. Her 5th Hail of Arrows is an empowered Hail of Arrows that deals higher DMG. Casting the ultimate gives 2 Arrows immediately.
  • Lithic Murmurs base — Assign to Growth Chamber to grant rare mineral growth rate +20/30%
  • Life in the Spores base — Assign to Growth Chamber to grant fungal matter growth rate +20/30%
  • Prey Checklist talent — Whenever a Nature Burst is triggered on an enemy, Typhoeus gains 1 Sign. DMG dealt by attacks empowered by consuming Arrows is increased to 1.2/1.4/1.6 times the original. When entering battle, Typhoeus immediately gains 1/2/4 Sign. Typhoeus can have up to 4 Arrows and 8 Signs. If Nature Burst is simultaneously triggered on multiple enemies, this only counts as one instance of Nature Burst when tallying the Signs gained by Typhoeus.
  • Huntress's Focus talent — When Hovering, Typhoeus activates a Protective Barrier that reduces her DMG Taken by 15/30% and makes her harder to interrupt while it is active. The Protective Barrier is shattered when Typhoeus takes DMG, and cannot be activated again for 30/18s afterwards.
