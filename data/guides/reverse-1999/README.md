## Sources

- Skills, rarity, afflatus, damage type: [BWIKI 神秘学家](https://wiki.biligame.com/reverse1999/) `{{神秘学家}}` templates fetched 2026-10-17. Incantations are rank 1 only. Insight I–III and the ultimate are included. Portray levels stay on the wiki page.
- Portraits and the character index: [Prydwen characters](https://www.prydwen.gg/re1999/characters), Last updated 11/October/2026. Images are the `cdn.prydwen.gg` URLs on that page.
- Tiers: [Prydwen tier list](https://www.prydwen.gg/re1999/tier-list), Overall - Euphoria, Last updated 11/October/2026. Editorial, not official. No pull advice is added.
- Teams: [Prydwen team tier list](https://www.prydwen.gg/re1999/team-tier-list), Last updated 08/October/2026. A line is kept only when every portrait slug maps to one card. `A | B` is one slot with the alternatives the page drew together. ` / ` separates slots.
- Banners: verified Weibo 重返未来：1999 (Sina mirror) for CN 4.0, and the Bluepoch Steam post for global 3.8. Clocks keep the timezone the post actually wrote.

## Stubs

6★ without a skill page: 德雷克 (`drake`), 狩勋女巫红弩箭 (`huntsworn-lilya`). Rarity and afflatus for those two are from the official CN posts. 德雷克 is not on the Prydwen index dated 11 October 2026, so there is no portrait. 狩勋女巫红弩箭 is on that index and on the Overall-Euphoria list (S+, Damage dealer); the card stays `stub: true` because the incantations are not copied.

5★ 阿夫西维 (`avksiviy`) has BWIKI skills and no Prydwen tier, so `summary.stub` is true. The id is a transliteration of the wiki's Cyrillic 英文名 Авксивий. No portrait was on the Prydwen index.

4★ without a skill page: 格林杜尔 (`glyndwr`). The CN post calls the character 4★ and does not print incantation numbers.

No rarity and no BWIKI template: `avgust`, `cheng-heguang`, `coppelia`, `cornerstone`, `enigma`, `everecho`, `hedone`, `lorentz-butterfly`, `ms-stranger`, `narcissus`, `nora`, `paper-heron`, `ramona`, `reed`, `rhiannon`, `silverwing-eagle`, `the-twins`. Prydwen lists the name, afflatus icon, and portrait. GameKee `reverse1999` search did not return skill articles for these names. Jina returned 403 for individual Prydwen character pages, so their kits are not copied.
